THE STORY BEHIND ROOKEES

A little idea.
A whole lot of heart.

Every brand starts somewhere. Rookees began with an idea: bringing two familiar treats together to create something worth sharing, remembering, and coming back to.

Made to be shared. Remembered for the taste. ✳

01 / THE BEGINNING

It started with a simple question. What if we brought two favourites together?

Brownies are loved for their rich, chocolatey goodness. Cookies bring their own familiar comfort, with a satisfying bite and a texture people know and enjoy.

Rookees explores the space between these two treats. The idea is simple: combine the character of brownies and cookies into one distinctive dessert concept, then learn what people enjoy most through real feedback.

Rather than assuming we already have all the answers, we want to build Rookees step by step — starting with the product, listening to people, and improving from there.

02 / THE IDEA

Familiar flavours. A different kind of treat.

Rookees is built around a brownie-cookie combination designed to feel indulgent, approachable, and fun. The product concept brings together rich chocolate character and the appeal of a classic cookie in a dessert worth trying.

But a good idea is only the beginning. Texture, sweetness, presentation, portion size, and overall value all matter. These are the details we want to keep exploring as Rookees develops.

03 / THE OPPORTUNITY

More than a sweet craving. An opportunity to create a brand.

We see room to explore a dessert brand that brings together product appeal, thoughtful presentation, and a close relationship with its customers.

01

A familiar category

Brownies and cookies are recognisable treats. Their familiarity gives us a starting point to introduce a combination that feels new without being difficult to understand.

02

A product worth sharing

Dessert can be part of a personal treat, a gift, or a shared moment. We want to explore packaging and presentation that make Rookees feel suitable for these occasions.

03

Room to keep improving

Customer feedback can help us test flavour preferences, pricing expectations, product presentation, and future variations before making bigger business decisions.

04 / LOOKING AHEAD

Built with curiosity. Growing with purpose.

Our vision is to develop Rookees into a recognisable dessert brand with a product people genuinely enjoy and a personality they can connect with.

The next steps are grounded in learning: refining the recipe, collecting honest feedback, understanding production costs, and discovering which customers and occasions fit the product best.

We don't believe growth should come before understanding. We want every next step to be informed by what we learn.

02

Listen to customers

03

Build sustainably

Evaluate pricing, costs, demand, and room to grow.
